# AWS MSP AI Hackathon

Compiled from the public official rules and FAQ, both captured on 2026-11-10. The theme is **Build the AI-First MSP: Run smarter, Deliver more.**

Three of the four uploaded files were the same rules clipping. This wiki keeps one raw copy of the rules and one raw copy of the FAQ. Where the FAQ and the rules could be read differently, the [official rules](aws-msp-judging.md#when-sources-differ) control.

## Tracks

Each submission picks one track. A person or team may enter more than one project when the projects are unique and substantially different.

| Track | Goal |
| --- | --- |
| Track 1 — Run Your MSP Better | Use AI to improve internal MSP operations, with measurable efficiency gains. Examples in the rules: automated triage and self-healing, multi-agent incident response, AI-driven FinOps. |
| Track 2 — Deliver AI-Driven Outcomes for Customers | Deliver differentiated AI-powered services to end customers. The rules label this track “External” and give three shapes: customer-facing AI solutions, business applications modernized with embedded AI, and new managed AI offerings with recurring revenue. |

Build requirements, the qualifying AWS AI services, and the submission checklist are on [What to build and how to submit](aws-msp-submission.md). Who may enter is on [Eligibility and registration](aws-msp-eligibility.md).

## Prize pool

The published total is **$64,000**. Each track has the same cash ladder: $10,000, $7,000, and $5,000 for first through third, plus five special prizes of $2,000. A project can win only one prize. Place winners (first through third) also get non-cash benefits. The amounts, the one-prize rule, and how prizes are paid are on [Judging, prizes, and ownership](aws-msp-judging.md).
