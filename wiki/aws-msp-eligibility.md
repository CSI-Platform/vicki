# Eligibility and registration

Who can enter the AWS MSP AI Hackathon, how registration works, and how the $200 AWS promotional credits are requested. Dates and the two tracks are on [AWS MSP AI Hackathon](aws-msp-hackathon.md).

## Who can enter

An entrant is an eligible individual, a team of eligible individuals, or an APN-registered organization.

An eligible individual is someone who, at the time of entry:

- is at least the age of majority where they live, and
- is employed by, or authorized to act for, an organization that has registered with the AWS Partner Network (APN) and signed the APN Terms & Conditions.

Holding the AWS MSP Program Validation is optional. The FAQ says so; the rules do not require it.

A team is eligible when every member is registered with the same AWS Partner. An organization entry can be a corporation, a nonprofit, an LLC, a partnership, or another legal entity, as long as it exists and is organized or incorporated at entry and is an APN partner that has signed the APN terms.

One eligible individual may join more than one team or organization, and may also enter alone.

Students and university-affiliated people can enter only when they also meet the employment or authorization test. An educational affiliation by itself is not enough.

## Registration

Register at <https://aws-agentic-msp.devpost.com/> with **Join Hackathon**. That requires a free Devpost account.

The registration form asks for:

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Corporate email | The address on the domain tied to the organization's APN registration |
| AWS Partner organization name | Required |
| Country of residence | Asked at registration (FAQ) |
| AWS SPMS ID | Optional (FAQ) |

The Devpost account email does not have to match the corporate email.

Registering consents to AWS and Devpost collecting and keeping personal information to operate and publicize the hackathon. Entrant information is also subject to the [Devpost privacy policy](https://info.devpost.com/privacy). AWS handles information under the AWS Privacy Notice. Publicity rights are on [Judging, prizes, and ownership](aws-msp-judging.md#ownership-and-promotion).

## Teams and organizations

A team or organization appoints one Representative. That person must be eligible, registers, submits, and acts for the group. By submitting, the Representative warrants they are authorized to act.

The Representative receives any prize and decides how to share it. See [How prizes are paid](aws-msp-judging.md#how-prizes-are-paid).

## Who cannot enter

- Residents of, or organizations domiciled in, a place where U.S. or local law prohibits participating or receiving a prize. The rules name Brazil, Quebec, Russia, Crimea, Cuba, Iran, and North Korea, plus any country comprehensively sanctioned by the U.S. Treasury's Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC).
- The sponsor, Devpost, and other organizations that design, produce, pay to promote, execute, or distribute the hackathon, and their parent companies, subsidiaries, and affiliates.
- Employees, representatives, and agents of those organizations, and the members of their immediate family or household.
- Anyone else involved in designing, producing, promoting, executing, or distributing the hackathon, and their immediate family or household.
- Any judge, and any company or individual that employs a judge.
- Anyone whose participation would, in the sponsor's or Devpost's discretion, create a real or apparent conflict of interest.

The rules define immediate family as a spouse, children and stepchildren, parents and stepparents, and siblings and stepsiblings. A household member is anyone who shares the residence for at least three months of the year. An agent, for this exclusion, is someone acting for a promotion entity under a contract or similar relationship. An affiliate is an organization under common control, common majority ownership, or common management, or one that substantially owns or is substantially owned by the other.

## Excluded places

Eligibility follows where the person lives, or where the organization is domiciled, not citizenship. A citizen of an excluded place who lives somewhere that is allowed can enter. A resident of an excluded place cannot enter or receive a prize, including when they are a citizen of an allowed place.

## AWS credits

While supplies last, a team can request **$200 in AWS Promotional Credits** for one AWS Partner AWS account.

| Rule | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Who can request | Someone registered for the hackathon who shows a valid submission idea |
| How many | One set of credits per team, applied to one AWS account ID. A partner organization can have more than one team, and each team can request one set |
| Request | [AWS Promo Credit form](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/01a17df24684796aa33f75c461d03210). Provide the last 4 digits of the AWS account ID. Do not request credits in Discord |
| Arrival | An email with a redemption code. Allow about one week. Do not resubmit while waiting |
| Expiry | Dec 31, 2026, 11:59 pm PT |
| What they cover | AWS services, including Kiro and the other products required to build in this hackathon. They are not cash |
| Account | An AWS account is required, and an AWS account has a payment method on file. Usage past the credits is billed to the entrant |
| Decline | AWS and Devpost may refuse credits when there is no valid submission idea |
| Region | Service availability differs by region and can change. Pick a region that has every service in the design before building. Check current AWS documentation |

Credit terms: <https://aws.amazon.com/awscredits/>.

These hackathon credits are part of how to enter. They are separate from the rule that disqualifies a project developed with other financial or preferential support from AWS or Devpost. That rule is on [Support that can disqualify a project](aws-msp-judging.md#support-that-can-disqualify-a-project).
