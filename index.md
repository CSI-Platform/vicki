# vicki wiki

A public demo of the LLM Wiki pattern: raw captures are compiled into interlinked markdown pages, and the route map makes the links visible.

## Wiki

- [Hulk inventory](wiki/hulk-inventory.md) — machine and software summary from the AppLens scan
- [Hulk USB and wireless connections](wiki/hulk-connections.md) — USB, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, serial, and network settings
- [AWS MSP AI Hackathon](wiki/aws-msp-hackathon.md) — theme, dates, sponsor, tracks, and the $64,000 prize pool
- [Eligibility and registration](wiki/aws-msp-eligibility.md) — who can enter, excluded places, and the $200 AWS credits
- [What to build and how to submit](wiki/aws-msp-submission.md) — qualifying AWS AI services and the submission checklist
- [Judging, prizes, and ownership](wiki/aws-msp-judging.md) — scoring, prize table, IP, and where to get help
